About Us

Our History

Government Jubilee Inter College is one of the oldest and finest government inter colleges in Lucknow. Many great personalities of the city studied here. The college offers education at High School and Intermediate level under the Uttar Pradesh Board, and is co-educational.

Its roots go back to the vision of Munshi Naval Kishor, the famous social worker of Lucknow, who dreamt of a secular, vernacular Anglo school for the city. With limited resources the dream looked hard — but not impossible — and it became a reality.
